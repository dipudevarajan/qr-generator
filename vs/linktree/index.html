adfreeqr.com / vs / linktree

Comparison · Updated June 2026

A free Linktree alternative — with a QR code.

Linktree popularised the "one link for everything" page. We made the same thing — unlimited, free, no ads, no sign-up — and added a printable QR code that points to it. Here's the honest side-by-side.

TL;DR

If you just want a multi-link page with a QR code and don't want to pay or sign up, use adfreeqr.com. If you want analytics dashboards, payment integrations, and a Linktree-branded URL, use Linktree.

What Linktree does well

Linktree is a polished product with millions of users. They have a clean editor, themes, payment integrations (Stripe, Square, PayPal), built-in analytics, and an ecosystem of integrations with Spotify, YouTube, Mailchimp, etc. The free tier is functional. If you're a creator monetising your audience and need payments + analytics in one place, Linktree is a legitimate choice.

What Linktree's free tier actually gives you

  • A page at linktr.ee/yourname — with "Join Linktree" branding at the bottom
  • Unlimited links
  • A handful of free themes (basic colors, no custom design)
  • Basic click counts (no detailed analytics)
  • No custom domain, no scheduling, no email/SMS capture, no SEO tools

You hit the paywall the moment you want to remove their branding ($5/month), use your own domain ($9/month), get detailed analytics ($24/month), or unlock most integrations.

What adfreeqr.com gives you

  • A multi-link page at adfreeqr.com/p/... — with only a small "Made with adfreeqr.com" footer
  • Unlimited links, unlimited pages
  • Full custom branding: any logo, any colors, any tagline
  • A printable QR code that points to your page
  • The option to download the HTML and host it on your own domain with zero changes
  • Open source under MIT — fork it, modify it, run your own copy

When to use adfreeqr.com instead of Linktree

  • You don't want to create an account just to share a few links
  • You want to fully brand the page without paying
  • You need a QR code that points to your link page (e.g. for a business card, restaurant menu, event poster)
  • You want to host the page on your own domain without paying $9/month
  • You're an open-source person and value being able to read the code

When Linktree is still the better choice

  • You're monetising directly through your link page (Stripe, PayPal, ticket sales)
  • You need granular per-link analytics with conversion tracking
  • You're using integrations like Mailchimp, Shopify, Spotify embeds extensively
  • You want a recognisable linktr.ee/... URL that your audience already trusts

The honest take: Linktree is great for creators running a business on their link page. adfreeqr.com is great for everyone else — small businesses, students, professionals, event organisers, anyone who just needs a clean multi-link page with a QR code and doesn't want to sign up or pay for it.

How the QR code piece works

This is the part Linktree's free tier doesn't have. When you build your multi-link page on adfreeqr.com, you also get a styled QR code (your colors, your logo in the centre) that points directly to it. Print it on a business card, a flyer, a restaurant table tent — anyone can scan it and land on your links page instantly.

If you ever want to switch off adfreeqr.com, you can download the link page HTML and host it on your own domain. The QR code keeps working as long as the URL it points to is alive.
